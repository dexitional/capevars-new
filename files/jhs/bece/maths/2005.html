  2. A circle has a circumference of 44 cm. Calculate its radius if π=227

    3. Solution

    Circumference =44 cm

    π=227

    Circumference of a circle =2πr

    Substituting the given values:

    ⇒44=2×227×r

    ⇒44=447×r

    ⇒44×7=447r×7

    ⇒44×7=44r

    Dividing both sides by the coefficient

    ⇒44×744=44r44

    ⇒r=7

    ∴ the radius is 7 cm.
