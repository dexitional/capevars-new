  2. Find the Least Common Multiple (L.C.M) of the following numbers: 3, 5 and 9.


    3. Solution

    Prime factors:

    3 = 3

    5 = 5

    9 = 3 × 3 = 3²

    Taking the highest powers of each prime:

    LCM = 32×5=9×5=45

    ∴ the LCM of 3, 5 and 9 is 45.



  3. If p+2qp=75, find the value of qp.



    4. Solution

    p+2qp=75

    Equating the denominators:

    ⇒p=5

    Equating the numerators

    ⇒p+2q=7

    ⇒5+2q=7

    ⇒2q=7−5

    ⇒2q=2

    ⇒2q2=22

    ⇒q=1

    ⇒qp=15
