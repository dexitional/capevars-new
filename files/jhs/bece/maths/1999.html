  1. Kofi and Kojo were given ₵38,000.00 to share. Kojo had ₵7,500.00 more than Kofi. Find,

    (i) Kofi's share

    (ii) Kojo's share

    2. Question 1a

    (i) Amount to be shared = ₵ 38,000

    Let Kofi's share =x

    then Kojo's share ₵=x+₵ 7,500

    ⇒ x + ₵(x+₵ 7,500) ₵=₵ 38,000

    ₵₵⇒2x+₵ 7,500=₵ 38,000

    ₵₵⇒2x=₵ 38,000−₵ 7,500

    ₵⇒2x=₵ 30,500

    ₵⇒2x2=₵ 30,5002

    ₵⇒x=₵ 15,250.00

    ∴ Kofi's share is ₵₵ 15,250.00



    (ii) Kojo's share
    ₵⇒x+₵ 7,500

    ₵₵⇒₵ 15,250+₵ 7,500

    ₵⇒₵ 22,750.00

    ∴ Kojo's share is ₵₵ 22,750.00
