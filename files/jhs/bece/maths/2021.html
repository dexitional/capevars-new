  2. The sum of two numbers is 81. If the second number is twice the first, find the second number.

    3. Solution

    let the first number =x

    Hence, the second number =2x

    ⇒x+2x=81

    ⇒3x=81

    ⇒3x3=813

    ⇒x=27

    Second number ⇒2x

    Second number ⇒2(27)

    Second number ⇒54

    ∴the second number is 54.―



  3. The floor of a rectangular hall is of length 9 m and width 4 m. How many tiles of 20 cm by 30 cm can be used to cover the floor completely?

    4. Solution

    1 m = 100 cm

    length of hall =9 m

    length of hall in cm

    ⇒ 9×100 cm

    ⇒ 900 cm


    width of hall =4 m

    width of hall in cm

    ⇒ 4×100 cm

    ⇒ 400 cm


    Area of rectangular hall in cm2

    ⇒length×width

    ⇒900 cm×400 cm

    ⇒360,000 cm2


    Area of one tile

    ⇒20 cm×30 cm

    ⇒600 cm2


    Number of tiles needed

    ⇒Area of rectangular hallArea of one tile

    ⇒360,000 cm2600 cm2

    ⇒600

    ∴ 600 tiles are needed to cover the floor completely.
