  2. A wire of length 38 cm is bent into the shape of a rectangle whose length is 7 cm more than the width. Find the area of the rectangle.

    3. Let the width of the rectangle, W ⇒x.

    Then the length of the rectangle, L ⇒x+7.

    The perimeter of the rectangle ⇒38 cm.

    Perimeter =2(L+W)

    Substituting the values:

    ⇒38=2(x+7+x)

    ⇒38=2(2x+7)

    ⇒38=4x+14

    ⇒38−14=4x

    ⇒4x=24

    ⇒4x4=244

    ⇒x=6

    ∴ The width of the rectangle is 6 cm, and the length is 6+7=13 cm.

    The area of the rectangle =length×width.

    ⇒ Area =13×6

    ⇒Area=78 cm2

    ∴ The area of the rectangle is 78 cm2.
