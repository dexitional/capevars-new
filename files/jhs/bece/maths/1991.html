  3. Given that m=−2 and n=34, find the value of

    (i) m2(n−1)

    (ii) n2−3m

    Solution

    m=−2

    n=34


    Question (3.c.i)

    m2(n−1)

    Substituting m and n into expression:

    ⇒(−2)2(34−1)

    ⇒4(34−1)

    Expanding the bracket:

    ⇒(4×34)−(4×1)

    ⇒3−4

    ⇒−1

    ∴ when m is −2 and n is 34, m2(n−1) is −1.


    Question (3.c.ii)

    n2−3m

    Substituting m and n into expression:

    ⇒(34)2−3−2

    ⇒(3242)+32

    ⇒916+32

    ⇒9 + 8(3)16

    ⇒9 + 2416

    ⇒3316

    ⇒2116

    ∴ when m is −2 and n is 34, n2−3m is 2116.
