  1. Kojo is n years old now.

    (i) How old was he 5 years ago?

    (ii) How old will he be 10 years from now?

    (iii) If his age in 10 years time will be four times his age 5 years ago, how old is he now?

    2. Watch the video below for a clearer understanding of the question.



    Question 1a

    Kojo's age now ⇒n

    (i) Kojo's age 5 years ago ⇒n−5

    (ii) Kojo's age 10 years years from now ⇒n+10

    (iii) n+10=4(n−5)

    ⇒n+10=4n−20

    ⇒20+10=4n−n

    ⇒30=3n

    ⇒3n3=303

    ⇒n=10

    ∴ Kojo is 10 years old now.

  3. Given that f=vuv+u, find v if f=20 and u=5

    4. Watch the video below for a clearer understanding of the question.



    Solution

    f=vuv+u

    Making v the subject:

    ⇒ f(v+u)=vuv+u×(v+u)

    ⇒ f(v+u)=vu

    ⇒ fv+fu=vu

    ⇒ fu=vu−fv

    ⇒ fu=v(u−f)

    ⇒ fuu−f=v(u−f)u−f

    ⇒ v=fuu−f

    If f=20 and u=5

    ⇒ v=20(5)5−20

    ⇒ v=100−15

    ⇒ v=20×5−3×5

    ⇒ v=−203

    ⇒ v=−623

    ∴ v is −623―







Question 2


  1. A man deposited ₵350,000.00 in his account at the bank. A simple interest of 4% per annum was paid on his deposit. Calculate the total amount at the end of 4 years.

    2. Solution

    Principal, P= ₵350,000.00

    Rate, R= 4%

    Time, T= 4 years

    Simple Interest, I=P×R×T100

    ⇒I = ₵₵350,000×4×4100

    ⇒I = ₵ 3500×16

    ₵⇒I=₵56,000

    ∴ the interest earned was ₵56,000.00 at the end of the 4 years

    Total amount after 4 years:
    ⇒ Principal + Interest

    ₵₵⇒₵350,000.00+₵56,000

    ₵⇒₵406,000.00

    ∴ the total amount at the end of the 4 years was ₵406,000.00.







  2. The cost of sending a telegram is ₵500.00 for the first 12 words and ₵25.00 for every extra word. Find the cost of sending a telegram containing 20 words.

    3. Solution

    Number of words = 20

    Cost of first 12 words = ₵500.00

    Remaining number of words:

    ⇒20−12

    ⇒8 words.

    Cost of remaining number of words:

    ₵⇒8×₵25

    ₵⇒₵200.00

    Total cost:

    ⇒ Cost of first 12 words + Cost of remaining 8 words

    ₵₵⇒₵500.00+₵200.00

    ₵⇒₵700.00

    ∴ the total cost of the 20 words is ₵700.00
