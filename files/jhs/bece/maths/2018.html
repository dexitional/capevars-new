  1. The sum of the interior angles of a regular polygon is 900∘. Find the number of sides of the polygon.

    Solution

    Sum of interior angles =900∘

    let the number of sides =n

    Sum of interior angles ⇒(n−2)×180∘

    ⇒(n−2)×180∘=900∘

    ⇒(n−2)×180∘180∘=900∘180∘

    ⇒n−2=5

    ⇒n=5+2

    ⇒n=7

    ∴ the polygon has 7 sides.
